# Resources For Design and Discovery of APIs

|:---|:---|:---|
| Abstract | Suite of utility API's (validate emails, create user avatars, calculate VAT, and more) | [Link](https://www.abstractapi.com) |

| Swagger | Tools for designing, building, and documenting RESTful APIs. | [Link](http://swagger.io/) |
