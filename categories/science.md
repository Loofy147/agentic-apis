# Science

| API | Description | Link |
|:---|:---|:---|
| MPDS | Provides experimental materials science data excerpted from the peer-reviewed publications. | [Link](https://developer.mpds.io) |
| NASA | Get NASA data, including imagery, list of asteroids based on their closest approach date to Earth and much more. | [Link](https://api.nasa.gov/index.html) |

| Open Access Button | Free, legal research articles delivered instantly or automatically requested from authors. | [Link](https://openaccessbutton.org/api) |
| SHARE | A free, open dataset of scholarly research activity across the scholarly lifecycle. | [Link](http://share-research.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) |
